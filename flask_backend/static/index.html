Pillar 1: Section 20(1) Personal Penalty Clock ₹250 / Day Mandatory

Under Section 20(1) of the RTI Act 2005, where the Commission finds that a Public Information Officer (PIO) has refused to receive an application or failed to furnish information within 30 days without reasonable cause, it shall impose a penalty of ₹250 each day until the information is furnished, up to ₹25,000 deducted directly from the officer's salary.

Precedent: Manohar s/o Manikrao Anchule v. State of Maharashtra AIR 2013 SC 681

Pillar 3: Section 6(3) 5-Day Mandatory Transfer Statutory 5-Day SLA

Where an application is made to a public authority requesting information held by another public authority, the receiving officer shall transfer the application within five days and immediately inform the applicant in writing.

Mandatory Nature of Section 20 Penalty

The apex court ruled that the penalty under Section 20(1) is mandatory upon finding unjustified delay, subject only to providing reasonable opportunity of hearing (audi alteram partem).

CBSE v. Aditya Bandopadhyay (2011) 8 SCC 497

Scope of Public Records & Inspection

Clarified that citizens have a right to inspect records, obtain certified copies, and that public authorities cannot deny access on broad pretextual claims of fiduciary relationship.

CIC v. State of Manipur (2011) 15 SCC 1

Section 18 Complaint vs Section 19 Appeal

Distinguished the punitive jurisdiction of Section 18 complaints (focusing on officer misconduct and penalties) from the remedial jurisdiction of Section 19 appeals (directing disclosure).
