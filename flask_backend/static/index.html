Frequently Asked Questions & Statutory Guidelines
How does ARZI cross-map IPC 1860 to Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023)?
ARZI includes a dual statutory parsing engine that recognizes old Indian Penal Code criminal clauses (e.g. IPC 420 for cheating, IPC 409 for criminal breach of trust by public servant) and automatically cross-indexes them to corresponding BNS 2023 substantive sections with maximum punishment scopes.
What is the Section 20(1) ₹250/day personal penalty clock?
Under Section 20(1) of the RTI Act 2005, when a Public Information Officer fails to furnish information within the 30-day statutory SLA without reasonable cause, the Central or State Information Commission is mandated to impose a penalty of ₹250 each day (up to ₹25,000) directly deducted from the officer's personal salary.
How does Notion Track two-way synchronization operate?
Your Python Flask code acts as the backend engine while Notion serves as the front-of-house interface. Advocates and desk officers review case dossiers inside Notion databases. The background poller detects state transitions, syncs dispatches, and records immutable timestamps in the Run Log.
Can advocates register custom State Acts and specialized legislation?
Yes. The Statutory Library module allows advocates to register any Central or State Act (such as Consumer Protection Act 2019 or State Revenue Codes). Once registered, custom acts can be cited in legal notices and active case dossiers.

Pillar 1: Section 20(1) Personal Penalty Clock ₹250 / Day Mandatory

Under Section 20(1) of the RTI Act 2005, where the Commission finds that a Public Information Officer (PIO) has refused to receive an application or failed to furnish information within 30 days without reasonable cause, it shall impose a penalty of ₹250 each day until the information is furnished, up to ₹25,000 deducted directly from the officer's salary.

Precedent: Manohar s/o Manikrao Anchule v. State of Maharashtra AIR 2013 SC 681
Pillar 2: IPC 1860 to BNS 2023 Criminal Codex Criminal Law Reform
• Land Mutation Fraud: IPC 420 / 218 → BNS 318(4) & 231
• Ration / Public Breach: IPC 409 / 166A → BNS 316(5) & 199
• Public Nuisance / Contamination: IPC 268 / 277 → BNS 270 & 279
Substantive Law: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Central Act 45 of 2023)
Pillar 3: Section 6(3) 5-Day Mandatory Transfer Statutory 5-Day SLA

Where an application is made to a public authority requesting information held by another public authority, the receiving officer shall transfer the application within five days and immediately inform the applicant in writing.
