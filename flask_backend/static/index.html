    RTI ACT 2005 KEY STATUTORY SECTIONS
    Section 2(f): Definition of 'Information' — Covers records, documents, memos, emails, contracts, reports, data material.
    Section 6(1): Filing of formal RTI application with prescribed fee (IPO/DD under Rule 3).
    Section 6(3): Mandatory 5-Day transfer window to the competent Public Authority.
    Section 7(1): Mandatory 30-Day SLA (or 48 Hours if life or liberty of person is involved).
    Section 7(6): Statutory entitlement to receive information FREE OF COST if SLA is breached.
    Section 19(1): Statutory First Appeal to the designated First Appellate Authority (Senior Officer).
    Section 20(1): Personal penalty of Rs. 250/day up to Rs. 25,000 and departmental disciplinary action.
